## What an AI chatbot actually does for a service business
First, it greets callers and asks the right qualifying questions—type of service, urgency, address—while the customer is still on hold. That information is instantly logged into your CRM, creating a lead before a human ever picks up. Second, the bot can schedule appointments in real time, pulling from your calendar and offering the next available slot. A dental office we built a bot for cut its front‑desk workload by 30 minutes per day and saw a 15% rise in new patient bookings because patients could lock in a time instantly. Third, the chatbot can handle routine follow‑ups, like confirming a technician’s arrival or sending a post‑service satisfaction survey, freeing staff to focus on the jobs that actually need a human brain.
