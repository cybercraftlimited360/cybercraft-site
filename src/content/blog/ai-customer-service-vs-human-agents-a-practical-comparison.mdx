# AI Customer Service vs Human Agents: A Practical Comparison

Picture this: a small HVAC company in Minnesota is receiving an influx of service calls during a heatwave. A typical AI chatbot is now equipped with a large language model that can handle a variety of customer queries with minimal supervision. For instance, a dental office might use an AI agent to answer frequently asked questions about office hours or insurance coverage, freeing up the front desk staff for more critical tasks.

## Is AI Suitable for All Types of Businesses?

AI is not a one-size-fits-all solution, and its effectiveness can vary across industries. For contractors and trades businesses, AI can handle routine calls, track job progress, and manage scheduling systems efficiently. Yet, the decision to deploy AI should be based on a careful analysis of your business needs and customer expectations.

For instance, in industries like healthcare, where sensitive information and empathy are crucial, human agents remain indispensable. AI can be an excellent tool for managing initial contact or triaging requests, but deeper, more meaningful interactions still require a human touch.
