## How Much Does a Virtual Receptionist Really Cost?
Virtual receptionists come with real human benefits but at a price. Typically, you're looking at monthly fees that range from $300 to $1,200, depending on the level of service and hours covered. If you need 24/7 availability, expect to be at the higher end of that spectrum. For instance, a dental office might spend around $700 monthly just to handle after-hours calls. Add training time and occasional errors, and the costs keep climbing.

## The Expense of AI Voice Platforms
Now let’s talk AI. An AI voice platform might seem like a big upfront investment, but the numbers often tell another story. The cost for such a system can start as low as $150 per month. AI solutions offer scalability without the added expense. Imagine a real estate team handling inquiries 24/7 without overtime pay—a game changer for their bottom line. Plus, AI doesn't call in sick or take vacations.

## Where Do You Get the Most Value?
When considering value, think beyond just the monthly fee. An AI system can process calls, schedule appointments, and even manage workflows. AI systems have matured to handle complex queries, thanks to advancements in large language models. In contrast, virtual receptionists might need scripts and can struggle with unexpected queries. ## Does AI Offer Reliability Over Human Touch?
While AI lacks the personal touch of a human, it compensates with consistency and availability. AI-driven systems are less prone to the errors that even the best virtual receptionists can make. For businesses like contractors, where missing a call can mean missing a job, AI ensures that no call goes unattended.

## Are There Hidden Costs with AI?
The setup phase for AI solutions might require an initial investment, but consider it a down payment on future savings. Unlike virtual receptionists, AI doesn’t require benefits packages or raises. For a business ready to integrate technology, the savings in operational costs can be significant. Plus, AI systems can be continually updated to improve efficiency without added expense.
