## What is AI Lead Qualification?
AI lead qualification is the process of using artificial intelligence to qualify leads and determine their potential for conversion. This is typically done through a series of questions and interactions with the lead, which are analyzed by a large language model to determine the lead's intent and potential for conversion. For example, an AI voice platform can be used to qualify leads by asking them a series of questions and then routing them to a human representative if they are deemed qualified.

The benefits of AI lead qualification are numerous. For one, it saves time and resources by automating the lead qualification process. This allows human representatives to focus on higher-value tasks, such as closing deals and building relationships with customers. Additionally, AI lead qualification can help to improve the accuracy of lead qualification, reducing the number of unqualified leads that are passed on to human representatives.

## How Does AI Lead Qualification Work?
AI lead qualification works by using a combination of natural language processing (NLP) and machine learning algorithms to analyze interactions with leads and determine their potential for conversion. This can be done through a variety of channels, including phone, email, and chat. For example, a cloud telephony infrastructure can be used to analyze phone calls and determine the intent of the caller.

The process typically starts with a lead interacting with a business through one of these channels. The interaction is then analyzed by a large language model, which determines the lead's intent and potential for conversion. If the lead is deemed qualified, they are routed to a human representative for further follow-up. If not, they may be routed to a workflow automation software for further nurturing.
