### What is an AI Phone Agent?

An AI phone agent is not just another piece of tech jargon; it's a sophisticated system that utilizes an AI voice platform to handle incoming calls. These agents are programmed to understand human language, much like a customer service representative. They can provide information, set appointments, and even escalate complex calls to a human operator if needed.

For plumbers, this means fewer missed opportunities and a more professional front for potential clients. Imagine a plumbing business where the AI agent directs calls to your team in the field, ensuring they only handle calls that require their expertise.

An AI agent can handle multiple calls simultaneously, answering frequently asked questions and booking appointments without human intervention.

Consider a typical plumbing business that receives around 50 calls per day. On average, each call might take about 3 minutes to answer.
