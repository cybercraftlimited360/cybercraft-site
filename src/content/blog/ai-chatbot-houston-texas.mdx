## How to Evaluate an AI Chatbot Provider

Not all AI chatbot providers are the same. Here's what to look for:

**Custom training, not templates** — the AI should be trained on your specific business, not a generic template with your logo on it. Ask to see a demo with your actual business info.

**Voice + chat, not just chat** — in 2026, the most effective AI systems handle both website chat and inbound phone calls. Leads come in from multiple channels and your AI should cover all of them.

**Integration with your existing tools** — the AI should connect to whatever calendar, CRM, or booking system you already use. If it requires replacing everything you have, that's a red flag.

**Transparent pricing** — you should know exactly what you're paying and what you get. Avoid providers that won't give you a straight answer on cost.

**Local support** — especially for Houston businesses, working with a provider who understands the local market makes a difference in how the system is built and trained.
