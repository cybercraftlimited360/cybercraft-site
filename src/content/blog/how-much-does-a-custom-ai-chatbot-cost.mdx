If you've been researching AI chatbots for your business, you've probably run into a lot of "contact us for pricing" pages and vague ranges like "$0 to $10,000+." Not helpful.

So here's the real answer — broken down by what you actually get at each price point.

## The Three Tiers of AI Chatbot Pricing

### Tier 1: Off-the-Shelf Tools ($0–$100/month)

Tools like Tidio, Intercom's basic plan, or ManyChat fall into this category. They're easy to set up and work fine for simple use cases — answering FAQs, collecting email addresses, routing visitors to the right page.

What they can't do:
- Know your specific products, pricing, and service area
- Handle nuanced questions about your business
- Integrate with your booking system, CRM, or internal tools
- Sound like your brand

If a customer asks "Do you guys service the Katy area on weekends?" a generic chatbot either gives a wrong answer or says "I don't know, please contact us." That's worse than no chatbot at all.

### Tier 2: Template-Based Custom Chatbots ($200–$800/month)

These are tools like Voiceflow or Botpress where an agency builds out a flow for your business. It's more customized than Tier 1 — they'll plug in your business info, your services, maybe connect it to a calendar.

It works better, but it's still rigid. The conversation has to follow a specific path. If a customer asks something the flow wasn't designed for, it breaks.

### Tier 3: Fully Custom AI ($500–$1,500/month)

This is what we build at CyberCraft360. It's a different category entirely.

A fully custom AI chatbot is:
- **Trained on your actual business data** — your services, pricing, FAQs, tone, how you handle edge cases
- **Conversational** — it can handle any question, not just the ones you anticipated
- **Integrated** — connected to your booking system, CRM, or whatever tools you use
- **Improving** — it learns from real conversations and gets better over time

The experience for a customer feels like talking to a knowledgeable member of your team, not clicking through a decision tree.

## What Drives the Price Up or Down

**Simpler = cheaper:**
- Single use case (e.g., just answering FAQs and booking appointments)
- Fewer integrations
- Lower call/chat volume

**More complex = higher investment:**
- Multiple use cases (chat + voice + follow-up)
- Deep integrations with existing systems
- High volume (thousands of conversations per month)
- Multilingual support

## Is It Worth It?

Here's how to think about the ROI:

If your business closes 10 deals a month at an average value of $500, that's $5,000/month in revenue. If a custom AI chatbot captures just 2–3 additional leads per month that you were previously losing to slow response times or missed after-hours inquiries — that's already paying for itself.

Most of our clients see the system pay for itself within 30–60 days.

## The Question You Should Actually Be Asking

Instead of "how much does it cost?" the better question is: **"How much is it costing me to not have this?"**

- How many calls go to voicemail after 6pm?
- How many leads fill out your contact form and never hear back within 5 minutes?
- How many hours does your team spend on repetitive questions that AI could handle?

That number is almost always bigger than the monthly cost of a custom system.
